# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales, profit, orders, product performance, and regional business performance using SQL Server and Microsoft Power BI.

The goal is to understand business performance, identify sales trends, evaluate profitability, and discover opportunities to improve business decisions.

The project combines SQL-based data analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard to present business findings in a clear and meaningful way.

## Business Objectives

- Measure overall sales, profit, and order performance.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories.
- Evaluate sales and profitability across regions.
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- Identify top-selling and most profitable products.
- Investigate loss-making products.
- Analyze profitability across product sub-categories.
- Evaluate business performance across discount ranges.
- Compare annual sales performance and year-over-year growth.
- Analyze sales, profit, and orders across shipping modes.

## Tools and Technologies

- **SQL Server:** Data querying and business analysis.
- **SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS):** Writing and executing SQL queries.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **SQL:** Aggregations, filtering, grouping, conditional logic, subqueries, and window functions.

### 3. Product Profit Analysis

## Key Business Findings

The analysis identified the following findings:

- **Category Performance:** Technology generated the highest category sales, approximately $836K.
- **Regional Profitability:** The West region generated the highest regional profit, approximately $108K.
- **Furniture Profitability:** Furniture generated approximately $18.9K in profit despite substantial sales.
- **Product-Level Losses:** Some products generated negative profit and may require further investigation.
- **Sales Trends:** Monthly sales and profit varied over time, highlighting the importance of monitoring business performance regularly.

*Note: The figures above are approximate and refer to the overall dataset before interactive filters are applied.*

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- Investigate pricing, discounts, and costs for loss-making products.
- Review Furniture category profitability to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Monitor regional performance to understand differences in profitability.
- Track monthly sales and profit trends to support business planning.
- Evaluate discount strategies to understand their relationship with profitability.
- Prioritize products and business areas that demonstrate sustainable profitability.
