# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales and profit data using SQL Server and Power BI. The objective is to understand business performance, identify sales trends, and find opportunities to improve profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)
- Microsoft Power BI
- SQL

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze total sales, profit, and orders.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories.
- Identify the most profitable regions.
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends.
- Identify top-selling and loss-making products.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Report Summary
- Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Orders KPI cards.
- Sales by Category.
- Profit by Region.
- Category and Region slicers.
- Key Business Insights.

### 2. Sales Analysis
- Sales by Region.
- Monthly Sales Trend.
- Monthly Profit Trend.
- Top 10 Products by Sales.

### 3. Product Profit Analysis
- Top 10 Most Profitable Products.
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit.
- Key Business Insights.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights
- Technology generated the highest category sales, approximately $836K.
- The West region generated the highest regional profit, approximately $108K.
- Furniture generated approximately $18.9K in profit despite substantial sales.
- Some products generated negative profit and may require further investigation.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Review pricing, discounts, and costs for loss-making products.
- Investigate the profitability of the Furniture category.
- Monitor monthly sales and profit trends.
- Use regional performance analysis to identify opportunities for improvement.
